# Bike-Store-Sales-Customer-Loyalty-Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a multi-store bike retail database to evaluate sales performance, customer behavior, and loyalty trends.
The objective was to transform raw transactional data into business insights that can support decision-making in areas such as revenue growth, retention strategy, and operational efficiency.

🎯 Business Problem

The retail company wants to understand:
Which products and categories drive the most revenue?
What is the repeat purchase rate?
Do certain categories influence customer loyalty?
How do stores and staff compare in performance?
Who are the high-value (VIP) customers?

🗂 Dataset Information

The dataset includes the following tables:

customers
orders
order_items
products
categories
brands
stores
staff

It represents transactional sales data from multiple stores between 2016–2018.

🛠 Tools Used

SQL (MySQL) – Data cleaning, aggregation, KPI calculation
Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard design

All KPIs were pre-aggregated using SQL queries and imported into Power BI for dashboard creation.

📊 Key KPIs Calculated

Total Revenue
Total Orders
Total Units Sold
Total Customers
Repeat Customers
Repeat Purchase Rate (%)

Top 10 Products by Revenue

Sales by Category and Brand
Store & Staff Performance

VIP Customer Identification

🔎 Key Insights

Total revenue reached $7.69M across 1,615 orders.
Repeat purchase rate was 9.07%, indicating opportunities for retention improvement.
Revenue is highly concentrated in specific brands.
Certain product categories show higher loyalty rates than others.
Store performance varies significantly, indicating potential operational optimization opportunities.

💡 Business Recommendations

Focus marketing campaigns on high-loyalty categories.
Develop retention strategies to improve repeat purchase rate.
Reduce dependency on dominant brands to diversify revenue.
Implement performance benchmarking across stores and staff.
